Key Features
SME Connect
A platform for Small and Medium business owners to engage more with existing and potential consumers and business associates. Create a business page, post photos and updates to reach a wider audience.
Talent Connect
A single platform for the Talent and event planners to network. Through Talent Connect, Business speakers, trainers and artists can market their profile and reach out to a wider target group and on the other hand, companies and individuals can connect with the Talent and hire them for their events.
PROjects
Post a task or project (like logo design, content writing etc) that you have been wanting to outsource and connect with multiple freelancers or subject matter experts. Choose the one who is best suited to finish your important task.
Jobs
Jobs on CONNECWRK aims to help job seekers in finding the right career opportunity and the recruiters get in direct contact with the prospective candidates. Recruiters can post Job openings on CONNECWRK, while Job seekers can browse and apply to relevant jobs.
